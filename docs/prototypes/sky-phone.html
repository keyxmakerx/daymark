Someone else's sky

Every sky grows from one person's own data and a seed of its own: the course of the river, the shapes it makes, and every colour in it. No two come out alike.

To see that, open Explore and tap Someone else's sky.

Every star is one of their memories. Drag to move, scroll or pinch to zoom, tap anything.

To make a constellation, tap Constellations on the bar at the bottom, then Draw a new constellation, tap the stars you want joined, and name it.

    How things form

    • Long bandDays in a row of doing your thing. Memories line up along it and gather into knots.
    • Small clustersOn-and-off weeks. Each run of days pulls together into its own little cluster.
    • SupernovaA life event you marked as a hard one. What came after gathers around it.
    • NebulaWeeks with a lot of writing. Every sky gets its own colours; try New colours.
    • Giant starsLife events. Zoom right in and they burn like a sun.
    • ColourNew memories are blue. Older ones turn white, gold, then red, and drift outward.
    • StreamsA couple of weeks in a row. The run stretches into a thin trail, like a comet.
    • ConstellationsYou draw them between your own stars and name them. If a star in one is put away, its outline stays, kept as a snapshot.
    • Binary starsTwo memories about the same person, slowly turning around each other.
    • CometSomething that comes back every year, like a birthday. It passes through on that day.
    • Meteor showerOn the anniversary of a day you starred, a few streaks fall from its star.
    • PulsarA habit you set a time for. It pulses on that beat.
    • Dark nebulaA cloud of cold dust, part of this sky's own scenery. It holds no memories.
    • Planetary nebulaA habit you chose to end. It finishes as a glowing ring, not a gap.
    • WhirlpoolA weekly habit kept for years. Its memories turn slowly around each other; each turn is about a year.
    • Dark matterMemories you tucked away to find again later. They don't shine, but the light behind them bends.
    • Black holeForms when you put memories away and draws them in slowly. Hidden, not deleted. It fades once nothing is left inside.
    • Ringed planetA place you keep going back to. Memories logged there ride around it as moons.
    • FavouritesMemories you starred carry a soft ring.
    • BridgeTwo runs of days close together, joined by the days that carried one into the next.
    • White holeForms when you bring memories back. It sends each one home, then fades.
